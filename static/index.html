About Us

We are an alliance of affiliated and disaffiliated UM churches and independent churches. Each member church’s Early Response Team is an autonomous unit that chooses to work alongside other Alliance members, so that together we can source manpower for multiple teams and serve on a sustained basis as the hands and feet of Christ.
